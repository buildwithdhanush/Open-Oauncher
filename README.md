Open-Launcher

Your Android. Your Workspace. Your Rules.

Open-Launcher is an open-source Android home screen launcher designed to provide extensive customization, flexible workspace management, powerful widgets, gestures, and productivity features.

Project Goals

- Freedom: No subscriptions, advertisements, or premium feature locks.
- Customization: Flexible layouts, themes, icons, gestures, and workspace configurations.
- Productivity: Integrated tools, shortcuts, panels, and widgets.
- Modularity: A maintainable architecture designed for long-term development.
- Privacy: No unnecessary accounts or data collection.
- Accessibility: A practical and responsive launcher experience across supported Android devices.

Development Status

🚧 Under active development. Features and compatibility will be documented as they are implemented and tested.
